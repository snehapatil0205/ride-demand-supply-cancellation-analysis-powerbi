# 🚗 Ride Demand–Supply & Cancellation Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes ride demand, supply patterns, and ride cancellations across multiple cities.

The dashboard helps identify peak demand hours, cancellation hotspots, and factors influencing ride cancellations.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze ride demand patterns across different hours
* Identify pickup zones with the highest ride cancellations
* Study cancellation rate trends
* Explore the impact of weather and time on ride demand

## 📊 Key Metrics

* Total Rides: 2500
* Cancelled Rides: 739
* Cancellation Rate: 29.56%

## 📈 Key Insights

* Ride demand increases significantly during peak hours.
* Certain pickup zones have consistently higher cancellation rates.
* Lower battery availability during peak hours may contribute to ride cancellations.
* Weather conditions can influence ride demand and supply balance.
